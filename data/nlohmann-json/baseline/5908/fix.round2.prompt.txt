# Pull request #5908: json_view: dump and read canada.json-style floats faster (batched float decisions)

## Description
1. **Read float tokens of 16 to 19 digits 16 bytes at a time.**
   - `layout_decimal()` combines the digits with NEON or SSE2 (`sixteen_digits()`), and `layout_float()` sends them to Eisel-Lemire directly.
   - `get<double>()` converts tokens of 16 or 17 digits without an exponent (the shape of coordinates written with full precision) with a lean Eisel-Lemire (`coordinate_float()`).
2. **Dump such tokens in batches of up to 256 ahead of the walk, one loop per step.**
   - The digits, 16 bytes at a time, and the token's last four digits.
   - `locate_token()`: Eisel-Lemire gives the double x. The position of x relative to the token is exact in a 64-bit fixed-point number, with 56 bits after the point of the token's last digit.
   - `token_digits_1/2()`, one loop per kind: the digits Zmij would write, chosen from that position. Exact ties between two candidates (like `754570942.33984373`, whose double is `...33984375`) are left to the full conversion.
   - The output is the token with its last four digits replaced. A rounding that carries further goes to the writer of Zmij's results.
   - The batch storage is allocated on the heap by the first conversion. A pair of floats like `[x, y]` in a read-only document is written without a frame.
   - Only on SIMD targets (NEON, SSE2) that are little-endian (`NLOHMANN_VIEW_VECTOR`, `NLOHMANN_VIEW_LITTLE_ENDIAN`). Other targets keep the previous code.
